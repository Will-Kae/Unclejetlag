## Tell us

Email [runway@unclejetlag.com](mailto:runway@unclejetlag.com) with the page URL, what's wrong and, ideally, an official source showing the correct information.

## What we do

1. We acknowledge reports as quickly as we can and prioritise **entry requirements, fees and safety information**.
2. We check the claim against primary sources.
3. If we got it wrong, we fix it promptly and update the page's **Last updated** date.
4. For significant factual corrections, we add a short correction note to the article explaining what changed.

## Minor changes

Typos, formatting and small clarifications that don't change meaning are fixed without a note.

## Rules that changed

If information was correct when published but the rule has since changed, we update it and treat it as an update rather than a correction.
