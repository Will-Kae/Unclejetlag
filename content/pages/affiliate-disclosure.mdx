## The short version

Some links on Uncle Jetlag are affiliate links. If you buy through them, Uncle Jetlag may earn a commission at no additional cost to you. We don't recommend products simply because they pay us.

Uncle Jetlag should be useful even if you never click one of these links. Commission never decides what we recommend or the order we show providers in.

## How it works

- Affiliate links are marked on the page, and articles containing them show a disclosure near the top.
- Outbound partner links go through `unclejetlag.com/go/…` and are tagged `rel="sponsored"` for search engines.
- We may also display advertising, such as Google AdSense. Ads are labelled "Advertisement".

## What partners can't do

Partners can't pay for a better ranking, review our articles before publication, or require positive coverage. If a partner's product gets worse, our recommendation changes.

## Labels we use

- **Affiliate partner**: we may earn a commission if you buy.
- **Editorial pick**: no commercial relationship.
- **Sponsored**: a partner paid for the placement. We don't currently run any sponsored placements.
- **Owned by our founder**: a business connected to Uncle Jetlag's founder.
