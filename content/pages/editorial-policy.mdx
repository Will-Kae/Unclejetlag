## Our promise

Practical, accurate, clearly dated travel information, written with personality, never at the expense of credibility.

## How we research

- **Primary sources first:** government immigration portals, embassy and consulate sites, official transport and tourism authorities, card-network and bank documentation, and provider terms.
- **Secondary sources** (news, forums, traveller reports) help us spot changes, but aren't treated as confirmation on their own.
- **Hands-on testing** for products such as eSIMs and travel cards before we recommend them.

## Verification and labelling

Every guide carries a status:

- **Verified:** checked by an editor against the sources listed on the page.
- **Editor review pending:** written, but not yet signed off. We don't let these pages be indexed by search engines.
- **Demo / placeholder:** shows the structure of a future guide. Unverified details are clearly marked "verify" or "placeholder" and must not be relied on.

Visa briefs show both a **Last updated** date and, where applicable, the date they were **checked against official sources**.

## Updates

Travel rules change. We review high-risk content (visas, entry rules, fees) more often than evergreen explainers, and we update the "Last updated" date only when content meaningfully changes.

## Independence

Advertisers and affiliate partners never review, approve or rank our content. Recommendations are made first; commercial relationships (if any) come second and are always disclosed. See our [Affiliate Disclosure](/affiliate-disclosure).

## Voice

Uncle Jetlag is the editorial voice of this publication. Humour is welcome; exaggeration about rules, costs or safety is not.
