## 4. Cookies, consent and your choices

We use necessary cookies to remember your preferences and, if you choose, allow certain third-party integrations. For details of cookie names, purposes, retention and controls, see our [Cookie Policy](/cookies).

- **Sitewide choice:** the cookie banner offers controls for optional analytics and advertising categories.
- **Partner tools:** hotel search, car rental search and the Genki calculator can be activated individually for the current visit without automatically granting sitewide advertising consent. A provider may then process information and set cookies under its own policies.
- **Flight-search analytics:** Travelpayouts' flight widget sets behavioural analytics identifiers. It requires a **separate affirmative choice**. Sitewide **Accept all** does not activate this permission.
- **Withdrawal:** use **Cookie settings** in the footer. You can turn off flight analytics independently or select **Essential only**. The website reloads to stop optional scripts and deletes specified first-party Travelpayouts cookies that it can control.

Withdrawal stops future processing based on that consent on our site, but cannot retrospectively undo processing already completed or erase cookies stored on another company's domain. You may also manage those cookies through your browser and the relevant provider.

## 5. Travelpayouts flight-search analytics

The flight-search widget is supplied by **Travelpayouts**. When you specifically choose **Allow and load flight search**, its embedded service may use Snowplow-style analytics to recognise a browser and analyse visits over time. On our site, testing identified:

- `_sp_id.*`: browser identifier and visit timestamps, approximately **13 months**;
- `_sp_ses.*`: visit/session identifier, approximately **30 minutes**.

The widget may connect to `tpwidg.com`, `avsplow.com`, `sentry.avs.io`, `img.wway.io` and `www.apistp.com`. These are observed integration hosts, not necessarily an exhaustive inventory of Travelpayouts' downstream processing.

We remember your separate allow/decline choice for up to **six months** in `uj_flight_analytics`. Declining does not load the widget. Withdrawing removes the first-party `_sp_*` cookies that our site can access and prevents the widget from reloading without permission. Travelpayouts' own privacy practices, recipients, transfer arrangements and retention may differ; please consult its policies or contact the provider directly.
