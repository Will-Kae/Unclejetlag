## What are cookies?

Cookies are small text files a website stores on your device. Similar technologies, such as local storage and pixels, work in similar ways. This policy covers all of them.

## Your choices

- **Change your mind at any time:** use **Cookie settings** in the site footer.
- **Until you choose**, Google tags on this site run with analytics and advertising storage set to **"denied"** (Google Consent Mode).
- **Your browser** can also block or delete cookies. Some site features may then stop working as intended.

## Advertising cookies (Google AdSense)

**AdSense is currently switched off:** the site doesn't load Google AdSense, and the cookies below aren't set. When we switch it on, we plan to fund the site with Google AdSense, and this section describes what will apply. **With your consent** where the law requires it, Google and its partners may set advertising cookies to serve, measure and personalise ads and to prevent fraud. These cookies can be set from google.com, doubleclick.net, googlesyndication.com or googleadservices.com.

| Cookie | Purpose (per Google) | Typical duration |
|---|---|---|
| `__gads` | Advertising, security | 13 months |
| `__gpi` | Advertising, security | 13 months |
| `__eoi` | Security | 6 months |
| `IDE` | Advertising, security (doubleclick.net) | 13 months in the EEA/UK; 24 months elsewhere |
| `DSID` | Security, functionality, advertising | 2 weeks |
| `test_cookie` | Checks whether your browser accepts cookies | 15 minutes |

Durations are Google's published values and may change. See Google's [list of advertising cookies](https://business.safety.google/adscookies/).

Without advertising consent, Google may still show **non-personalised or limited ads**. You can manage personalised ads at Google's [Ads Settings](https://adssettings.google.com/) and learn more at [How Google uses information from sites that use its services](https://policies.google.com/technologies/partner-sites).

## Flight search analytics (Travelpayouts)

Our flight search is provided by Travelpayouts. When it loads, Travelpayouts runs **behavioural analytics** that we can't switch off, so the search asks for your permission separately and loads only if you allow it. This permission is separate from advertising consent: "Accept all" doesn't include it, and allowing it doesn't switch on anything else.

| Cookie | Set by | Stored on | Purpose | Duration |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| `_sp_id.<4 characters>` | Travelpayouts analytics (loaded by the flight search) | `unclejetlag.com` | A unique ID for your browser and the times of your visits | About 13 months |
| `_sp_ses.<4 characters>` | Travelpayouts analytics | `unclejetlag.com` | Tracks the current visit | 30 minutes |

When the flight search loads, your browser also connects to Travelpayouts services, including `tpwidg.com`, `avsplow.com`, `sentry.avs.io`, `img.wway.io` and `www.apistp.com`. What Travelpayouts does with this data is covered by Travelpayouts' own privacy and cookie policies.

- **Allow:** press **Allow and load flight search** under the search, or tick **Flight search analytics** in Cookie settings. We remember your choice for 6 months.
- **Decline:** press **No thanks**, or leave the box unticked. Nothing is loaded from Travelpayouts and these cookies aren't set.
- **Withdraw at any time:** press **Turn off** under the flight search, untick the box in Cookie settings, or choose **Essential only**. The page reloads without the search, and we delete `_sp_id.*` and `_sp_ses.*` from our domain. If either cookie is ever found without your permission, we delete it on the next page load.
