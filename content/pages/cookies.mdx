## 1. Scope and technologies

Cookies are small text files stored by your browser. Related technologies include pixels, scripts, embedded frames and local storage. This policy explains the technologies used by Uncle Jetlag and the partners you choose to activate, their purposes, retention and your controls. Read it alongside our [Privacy Policy](/privacy) and [Affiliate Disclosure](/affiliate-disclosure).

Some cookies are first-party (stored on the website you visit); others belong to providers on their own domains. We can remove identified first-party tracking cookies under our control, but generally cannot delete cookies set on another provider's domain.

## 2. Your choices and consent

- **Change your mind at any time:** use **Cookie settings** in the site footer.
- **Until you choose**, Google tags on this site run with analytics and advertising storage set to **"denied"** (Google Consent Mode).
- **Your browser** can also block or delete cookies. Some site features may then stop working as intended.

## 7. Flight search analytics (Travelpayouts)

Our flight search is provided by Travelpayouts. When it loads, Travelpayouts runs **behavioural analytics** that we can't switch off, so the search asks for your permission separately and loads only if you allow it. This permission is separate from advertising consent: "Accept all" doesn't include it, and allowing it doesn't switch on anything else.

| Cookie | Set by | Stored on | Purpose | Duration |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| `_sp_id.<4 characters>` | Travelpayouts analytics (loaded by the flight search) | `unclejetlag.com` | A unique ID for your browser and the times of your visits | About 13 months |
| `_sp_ses.<4 characters>` | Travelpayouts analytics | `unclejetlag.com` | Tracks the current visit | 30 minutes |

When the flight search loads, your browser also connects to Travelpayouts services, including `tpwidg.com`, `avsplow.com`, `sentry.avs.io`, `img.wway.io` and `www.apistp.com`. What Travelpayouts does with this data is covered by Travelpayouts' own privacy and cookie policies.

- **Allow:** press **Allow and load flight search** under the search, or tick **Flight search analytics** in Cookie settings. We remember your choice for 6 months.
- **Decline:** press **No thanks**, or leave the box unticked. Nothing is loaded from Travelpayouts and these cookies aren't set.
- **Withdraw at any time:** press **Turn off** under the flight search, untick the box in Cookie settings, or choose **Essential only**. The page reloads without the search, and we delete `_sp_id.*` and `_sp_ses.*` from our domain. If either cookie is ever found without your permission, we delete it on the next page load.

## 8. Affiliate links and third-party cookies

When you click an affiliate link (routed through `unclejetlag.com/go/…`), you leave our site. The partner may set its own cookies to record the referral. Those cookies are covered by the partner's own cookie and privacy policies. See our [Affiliate Disclosure](/affiliate-disclosure).

## 9. Retention and limitations

The lifetimes above are observed or provider-published estimates and may change. Browsers may clear cookies sooner. Blocking a partner tool may prevent that specific search or calculator from working, while our travel guides remain available.

The website stores sitewide choices in `uj_consent` and a separate allow-or-decline flight analytics decision in `uj_flight_analytics`, each for up to 180 days. A declined choice does not authorise tracking. Activating a hotel, car-rental or Genki tool is a visit-specific choice, not blanket advertising permission.

Withdrawal reloads the page to prevent further optional integrations and removes identified Travelpayouts first-party cookies for the withdrawn category. It cannot undo processing that already occurred or remove cookies held on partner-owned domains. You may also clear cookies using your browser settings.
