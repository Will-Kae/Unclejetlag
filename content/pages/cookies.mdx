## What are cookies?

Cookies are small text files a website stores on your device. Similar technologies, such as local storage and pixels, work in similar ways. This policy covers all of them.

## Your choices

- **Change your mind at any time:** use **Cookie settings** in the site footer.
- **Until you choose**, Google tags on this site run with analytics and advertising storage set to **"denied"** (Google Consent Mode).
- **Your browser** can also block or delete cookies. Some site features may then stop working as intended.

## Advertising cookies (Google AdSense)

We use Google AdSense to fund the site. **With your consent** where the law requires it, Google and its partners may set advertising cookies to serve, measure and personalise ads and to prevent fraud. These cookies can be set from google.com, doubleclick.net, googlesyndication.com or googleadservices.com.

| Cookie | Purpose (per Google) | Typical duration |
|---|---|---|
| `__gads` | Advertising, security | 13 months |
| `__gpi` | Advertising, security | 13 months |
| `__eoi` | Security | 6 months |
| `IDE` | Advertising, security (doubleclick.net) | 13 months in the EEA/UK; 24 months elsewhere |
| `DSID` | Security, functionality, advertising | 2 weeks |
| `test_cookie` | Checks whether your browser accepts cookies | 15 minutes |

Durations are Google's published values and may change. See Google's [list of advertising cookies](https://business.safety.google/adscookies/).

Without advertising consent, Google may still show **non-personalised or limited ads**. You can manage personalised ads at Google's [Ads Settings](https://adssettings.google.com/) and learn more at [How Google uses information from sites that use its services](https://policies.google.com/technologies/partner-sites).

Pressing a **Load** button loads only that one tool, for your current visit. It isn't saved and doesn't switch on advertising cookies. Once a tool has loaded, its provider may set its own cookies, which are covered by that provider's cookie and privacy policies. If you withdraw advertising consent in **Cookie settings**, the page reloads without these scripts, and we delete the Travelpayouts tracking cookies stored on our own domain (`_sp_id.*`, `_sp_ses.*` and `am_user_session`). Cookies a provider has set on its own domain stay in your browser until they expire or you delete them.
