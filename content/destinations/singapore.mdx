## Overview

Singapore is a compact city-state that works smoothly: the Marina Bay skyline, Gardens by the Bay, hawker centres, Chinatown, Little India and Sentosa. It is one of the easiest places to visit in Asia, and it is a major connecting hub for travel across the region.

The rules are strict and clearly signposted. Know them before you arrive, and the city rewards you with efficiency and good food at every price.

## Entry requirements

Visa rules depend on your passport, so check the Immigration and Checkpoints Authority (ICA) before you book. Many nationalities can enter without a visa for a short stay, and others need to apply in advance.

Most visitors must submit the **SG Arrival Card** online, free of charge, within a short period before arrival. Passports generally need at least six months of validity, and you may be asked to show onward travel.

Singapore has **strict laws** on drugs, vaping devices and some medicines, and penalties are severe.

## Best time to visit

Singapore is hot and humid all year, with temperatures that vary little. February to April tends to be drier, while the northeast monsoon from November to January brings more rain. Plan outdoor sightseeing for mornings and evenings, and use air-conditioned malls and museums at midday.

## Money

The currency is the **Singapore dollar (SGD)**. Cards and contactless payments are widely accepted, including at many hawker stalls, and ATMs are everywhere. Tipping is not expected, as a service charge is often added at restaurants. Prices on menus may exclude service charge and GST, so check the bottom of the menu.

For everyday spending, compare [the best ways to pay while travelling](/money/best-ways-to-pay-while-traveling) and always choose the local currency when a terminal offers to convert ([why](/money/pay-in-local-currency-or-home-currency)).

## Mobile data and eSIMs

Singtel, StarHub and M1 sell tourist SIMs at Changi Airport and in shops. Coverage is excellent. A travel eSIM is easy and works well for a short stay.

## Getting around

The **MRT** metro and buses are efficient and cheap, and you can tap a Visa or Mastercard contactless card or phone at the gates in most cases. Taxis and ride-hailing are plentiful. Singapore is walkable and well shaded, though the heat makes short taxi rides tempting. Singapore drives on the **left**.

## Safety and health

Singapore is considered very safe, but rules are enforced: littering, jaywalking and smoking in banned areas draw fines, and drug offences carry severe penalties. Do not bring vapes or e-cigarettes. Dress for the heat, drink water and carry a small umbrella for sudden rain.

## Things to do

- Gardens by the Bay and the Marina Bay skyline
- Hawker centres such as Maxwell and Lau Pa Sat
- Chinatown, Kampong Glam and Little India
- Sentosa, the Singapore Zoo and Night Safari
- Changi Airport's Jewel

## Airports

**Changi (SIN)** is regularly rated among the world's best airports and has an MRT station for quick city access. Allow time for your SG Arrival Card, and check onward travel requirements if transiting.

## Local customs

Queue and keep to the left on escalators. Remove your shoes at homes and some temples. Public behaviour is orderly, and loud noise or eating on public transport is discouraged. Hawker centres are casual, and you can reserve a table with a packet of tissues.

## Emergency numbers

Police: **999**. Ambulance and fire: **995**.
