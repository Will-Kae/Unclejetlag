## Overview

South Africa rewards people who plan: huge distances, big-city contrasts, world-class wildlife and wine, and a tourism infrastructure that's excellent when you know how it works. Johannesburg is the main air gateway to the region; Cape Town is the postcard.

## Entry requirements

Entry rules depend on your passport. Some nationalities are visa-exempt for short visits; others must apply in advance.

- **Visa exemptions** are set per passport, usually **30 or 90 days**. Check DIRCO's official list for yours. Zimbabwean and many other SADC passports get 90 days, for example; see our [Zimbabwe → South Africa brief](/visas/zimbabwe/south-africa).
- **Passport:** valid for at least **30 days after you leave** South Africa, with at least **2 consecutive completely blank visa pages**. Travellers without these are refused boarding or entry.
- **Yellow fever:** if you arrive from a yellow-fever-risk country, you must show a valid vaccination certificate ("yellow card"). The vaccine must be given at least 10 days before arrival.
- **Children:** extra documents can be requested for minors, such as birth certificates and parental consent. Check Home Affairs guidance before travelling with under-18s.
- **Cash:** you can carry up to **R100,000** in foreign or local currency without prior approval (raised from R25,000 in 2026). Declare anything above that.
- **Traveller declaration:** since 1 July 2026, everyone entering or leaving must complete a SARS online traveller declaration, no more than 24 hours before travel. [What changed](/guides/south-africa-online-traveller-declaration-r100000-cash-limit).

## Visa information

Visa applications and exemptions are administered by the [Department of Home Affairs](https://www.dha.gov.za). Use only official channels and be wary of intermediaries promising "guaranteed" visas.

## Best time to visit

- **Cape Town & the Western Cape:** warm, dry summers (roughly November to March); winters are cooler and wetter.
- **Kruger & safari areas:** the drier winter months are commonly recommended for game viewing, as vegetation thins and animals gather near water.
- **Durban & KwaZulu-Natal:** mild year-round, humid in summer.

## Currency

The **South African rand (ZAR, R)**. Cards are the default in cities; keep small notes for tips, parking attendants and informal vendors.

## Typical daily budget

Per person per day, sharing a room, including food and local transport (2026 estimates):

| Style | Per day | What it looks like |
|---|---|---|

| Mid-range | R2,500–5,000 (≈US$140–290) | Guesthouses and 3–4★ hotels, restaurant meals, hire car, paid attractions |
| Comfort / safari | R8,000+ (≈US$450+) | Private game lodges (often all-inclusive), boutique hotels, domestic flights |

**Reference prices:**

- Car hire: roughly US$22–70 a day.
- Uber across central Cape Town: a few dollars.
- Return domestic flight Cape Town–Johannesburg: about US$120–230.
- Winter (May–August) rates are often 30–40% lower than peak summer and December holidays.

**The rand moves a lot, so check live exchange rates when you budget.**

## Cards vs cash

Card acceptance is excellent in cities, malls, restaurants and hotels, and contactless is common. Tipping restaurant staff and fuel-station attendants is customary, which is where small cash helps.

## ATMs

Use ATMs inside malls, banks or busy, well-lit areas. Don't accept "help" from strangers at an ATM, and shield your PIN.

## Mobile data & eSIMs

Local SIMs are sold widely; South African law requires SIM registration (RICA), so bring your passport and proof of address details (your accommodation address is typically used). Travel eSIMs avoid the paperwork.

The four networks are **Vodacom**, **MTN**, **Cell C** and **Telkom**. Vodacom and MTN have the best rural coverage for road trips and safaris.

- **SIM cards** cost a few rand at airport kiosks, operator stores and supermarkets. Registration (RICA) needs your **passport** and sometimes your accommodation address.
- **Data bundles:** a month of data starts around **R100–170 for about 5 GB**, depending on the operator and promotion.
- **Airport kiosks** are convenient but usually steer you to pricier bundles.

## Transportation

- **Ride-hailing apps** are widely used in major cities and are the default for many visitors.
- **Gautrain** connects O.R. Tambo airport with Sandton, Rosebank and Pretoria. It's the safest and fastest airport link in Johannesburg. Airport trips carry a surcharge, so check current fares on gautrain.co.za.
- **MyCiTi** buses in Cape Town run between the airport and the city centre and along the Atlantic seaboard.
- **Driving** gives the most flexibility for the Garden Route and safari parks. South Africa drives on the **left**.

## Accommodation

From city boutique hotels to game lodges and self-catering cottages. Private game reserves are often all-inclusive; national park rest camps are more affordable and book up early in peak seasons.

## Safety considerations

South Africa requires more active safety habits than many destinations: don't display valuables, keep car doors locked and windows up in traffic, avoid walking in isolated areas after dark, and take local advice about specific neighbourhoods.

Specific risks flagged by the UK government:

- **Being followed from O.R. Tambo airport** and robbed on arrival, sometimes at gunpoint. Use the Gautrain or a pre-booked transfer, and stay alert when you arrive at your accommodation.
- **Carjacking**, especially after dark and at traffic lights, junctions, petrol stations and driveways. Criminals sometimes pose as police using blue lights.
- **ATMs:** use them inside malls or banks, and refuse "help" from strangers.
- **Table Mountain:** hikers have been attacked. Hike in groups of four or more, on busy trails only.
- **Protests** can turn violent at short notice.
- **Townships:** visit with a registered guide.

## Travel insurance

Private healthcare in South Africa is high quality but expensive, so make sure your policy includes medical cover and evacuation, especially for safari regions.

## Things to do

- Table Mountain, the Cape Peninsula and Cape Point
- The Cape Winelands (Stellenbosch, Franschhoek)
- Kruger National Park and neighbouring private reserves
- The Garden Route road trip
- Johannesburg's Apartheid Museum and Constitution Hill

## Airport information

**O.R. Tambo (JNB)** is the main international hub; **Cape Town (CPT)** has good long-haul and regional connections; **King Shaka (DUR)** serves Durban. Domestic flights are often the practical way to cover long distances.

## Useful apps

A ride-hailing app, an offline map, your bank's app, and an app or radio station for **load-shedding** (scheduled power cuts) updates if they're in effect during your trip.

## Local customs

Tipping is expected in restaurants and for many services. "Now now" means soon-ish; "just now" means later. People are generally friendly and direct.

## Emergency information

Police **10111**, ambulance **10177**, or **112** from a mobile phone. Many private security and ambulance services also operate; your accommodation can advise.
