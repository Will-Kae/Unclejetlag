**What changed:** From **12 November 2026**, every passenger arriving in Ghana must complete the Ghana Health Service (GHS) health declaration online before travelling. The GHS announced the rule in a press release on 7 November, following an earlier Ministry of Health directive on Ebola screening. When you submit the form you get a QR code, which you show to the Port Health officer at your point of entry.

## Who it affects

All arriving travellers, whatever their nationality, including visitors from South Africa, Zimbabwe, the rest of Africa, the UK, the EU and the US. A parent or guardian completes the form for a child under 12. The Ministry of Health says Ghana has not recorded a confirmed Ebola case and describes the measures as precautionary.

## What the form asks

The form has six steps covering passenger details, travel information, health screening, accommodation and a final declaration. You are asked about travel to or through Ebola-affected countries in the previous 21 days, symptoms, and any contact with suspected or confirmed cases. It is available in English and French.

## Key dates

- **3 November 2026:** Ministry of Health announces mandatory declarations and enhanced screening at Accra, Kumasi (Prempeh) and Tamale (Yakubu) international airports.
- **7 November 2026:** GHS confirms the declaration is mandatory for all arrivals.
- **12 November 2026:** requirement takes effect. No end date has been given; the Ministry says it will be reviewed as the situation evolves.

<Callout type="warning" title="Use the official site only">
The current form is at ehds.ghs.gov.gh. Older pages from the Covid era, and third-party sites offering to file it for you, are not the official route.
</Callout>

## What to do now

1. Complete the declaration at ehds.ghs.gov.gh before you leave for Ghana, and save or screenshot the QR code.
2. Expect temperature checks and possible extra questions at Port Health on arrival.
3. Keep your contact details up to date, as Port Health may contact you within 21 days of arrival.
4. If you have fever or other symptoms, the Ministry advises postponing travel and seeking medical care.
5. Confirm the latest requirements with the Ghana Health Service and your airline before you travel.
