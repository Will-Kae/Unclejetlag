Georgia is still one of the best-value trips in the wider European region. It also isn't as cheap as some older blog posts suggest: hotel prices in Tbilisi and Batumi have risen, and wine is marked up hard in restaurants.

This guide costs a realistic **14-day route** using **2026 prices** from Georgian transport operators and recent on-the-ground price guides.

## The route (14 days, 13 nights)

1. **Tbilisi (4 nights):** old town, sulphur baths, food and wine bars
2. **Kakheti (2 nights):** Sighnaghi, Telavi and wineries
3. **Kazbegi / Stepantsminda (3 nights):** Gergeti Trinity Church and mountain hikes
4. **Kutaisi (2 nights):** Prometheus Cave, Martvili Canyon, Gelati Monastery
5. **Batumi (2 nights):** Black Sea coast. Fly out of Batumi, or return to Tbilisi by train

## What things actually cost

**Accommodation:**

- Hostel dorm: US$8–15
- Guesthouse double: US$30–45
- 3★ hotel: US$55–75
- Kakheti family guesthouse with dinner and breakfast: US$20–35 per person
- Batumi beachfront hotel in peak season: US$60–120 a room

**Getting around:**

- Tbilisi metro or bus: **₾1**, with 90 minutes of free transfers when you pay by Metromoney or contactless card
- Bolt across central Tbilisi: ₾7–15
- Marshrutka from Tbilisi to Sighnaghi: about ₾10
- Marshrutka from Tbilisi to Kazbegi: ₾10–15
- Marshrutka from Tbilisi to Batumi: about ₾25
- Train from Tbilisi to Batumi: ₾20–35

**Sights and experiences:**

- Prometheus Cave: about ₾23
- Martvili Canyon (boat and walkway): about ₾25
- Private room at the Tbilisi sulphur baths: US$30–60 an hour, split between your group
- Guided Kakheti wine tour: US$45–65 per person

**Food and drink:**

- Khachapuri from a bakery: a few lari
- Dinner for two at a good restaurant: ₾60–80
- Cappuccino: ₾8–10
- Glass of Georgian wine: ₾5–15

## Costs people forget

- **Mandatory travel insurance:** since 1 January 2026, you need at least **GEL 30,000** of medical cover to enter Georgia.
- **Visa or e-visa fees**, if your passport needs one. The e-visa costs about US$20; see the [Zimbabwe → Georgia brief](/visas/zimbabwe/georgia) for how it works.
- **ATM and foreign-transaction fees.** See [how FX fees work](/money/how-foreign-exchange-fees-work).
- **Kutaisi airport transfers.** Low-cost flights often land in Kutaisi, 3–4 hours by road from Tbilisi.
- **Wine markups.** Restaurants often charge 3–4× the shop price. Wine bars and cellar doors are far better value.
- **Tips.** Some restaurants add a service charge to the bill. Check before tipping again.

<Callout type="tip">
  **Save 20–30%:** travel in May–June or September–October. The weather is better for wine country, hotel rates in Tbilisi and Batumi drop, and mountain guesthouses are still open.
</Callout>

## Money tips for Georgia

- **Pay in lari.** If a card machine or ATM offers to charge you in your home currency, decline. [Here's why](/money/pay-in-local-currency-or-home-currency).
- **Carry cash** for marshrutkas, markets and village guesthouses.
- **Cards** work widely in Tbilisi and Batumi.
