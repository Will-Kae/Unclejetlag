**The short version:** ETIAS, the online travel authorisation for visa-free visitors to most of Europe, is **not live yet**, and as of 28 September 2026 the EU has **not announced an exact start date**. The official target is still the **end of 2026**.

## What's official

- **eu-LISA**, the EU agency building the system, says the next steps point towards the **launch of ETIAS by the end of 2026**.
- The EU's plan, reported by Fragomen, places the launch in the **last quarter of 2026**, followed by a **transitional period** and a **grace period** before the authorisation is strictly enforced.
- ETIAS applies to travellers who **don't need a visa** for the Schengen area. If you already need a Schengen visa, ETIAS doesn't apply to you.

## What's not confirmed

Some unofficial ETIAS websites now say the launch has slipped to 2027. **The EU hasn't confirmed that.** Treat any site asking you to pay for ETIAS now as a red flag: the system isn't open for applications.

<Callout type="warning" title="Beware of fake ETIAS sites">
  When ETIAS opens, apply only through the official EU website or app. Unofficial sites charge extra fees and some are scams.
</Callout>

## What to do now

1. **Nothing to apply for yet.** Travel to Europe as you do today.
2. Check your **passport validity**. ETIAS will be linked to your passport.
3. We'll post an update as soon as the EU announces a date. Follow [Uncle Jetlag updates](/updates) or the [RSS feed](/updates/rss.xml).
