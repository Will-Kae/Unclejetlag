"Do I need a visa?" has more possible answers than yes or no. Understanding the categories helps you read official websites correctly and avoid expensive misunderstandings.

## Real examples of each type

- **Electronic travel authorisation (UK):** visa-exempt visitors to the UK need an **ETA**, which costs **£20**. It isn't a visa. It's a pre-travel check. See our [UK guide](/destinations/united-kingdom).
- **Electronic travel authorisation (Canada):** visa-exempt air travellers need an **eTA**, which costs **CAN$7**. Travellers who need a visa apply for a visitor visa instead. See our [Canada guide](/destinations/canada).
- **ETIAS (Schengen area):** the EU's authorisation for visa-free nationals is **scheduled for the last quarter of 2026**. Once approved, it's valid for **3 years** or until your passport expires. The **Entry/Exit System (EES)**, which records your fingerprints and photo at the border, has been operational since 10 April 2026.
- **e-Visa (Georgia):** issued online at the official **evisa.gov.ge** portal. See [Zimbabwe → Georgia](/visas/zimbabwe/georgia).
- **Consular visa (UK Standard Visitor):** visa nationals apply online, then give biometrics at a visa application centre. See [South Africa → UK](/visas/south-africa/united-kingdom).
- **Visa-free (South Africa):** some passports, including Zimbabwe's, can enter visa-free for a limited stay. See [Zimbabwe → South Africa](/visas/zimbabwe/south-africa).

<Callout type="warning" title="Beware lookalike sites">
  Search results for "ETA", "eTA", "ETIAS" and "e-visa" are full of third-party sites that charge several times the official fee. Apply only through government domains such as gov.uk, canada.ca and evisa.gov.ge, and for ETIAS, only via the official EU channels once it launches.
</Callout>

## Single, double and multiple entry

A **single-entry** visa ends when you leave. **Multiple-entry** visas let you leave and come back within the validity period, which is useful for regional trips that loop back through a hub.

## Validity vs length of stay

These are different numbers. Read both carefully on any visa you receive.

**Example:** in the Schengen area, short stays are limited to **90 days in any 180-day period**. That applies however long your visa itself is valid for.

## Documents almost every visa asks for

- A passport with enough validity and blank pages. See [the six-month rule](/visas/guides/passport-validity-six-month-rule).
- Proof of funds, such as recent bank statements
- Accommodation and return or onward travel bookings
- Travel medical insurance (mandatory for Schengen visas, and for all visitors to Georgia since 1 January 2026)
- A purpose-of-trip document, such as an invitation letter, conference registration or employer letter

## Airside vs landside transit

- **Airside transit:** you stay in the international zone. Some nationalities still need a transit visa for this in some countries.
- **Landside transit:** you pass through immigration (e.g. to change airports or collect bags), which may require a visa.

<Callout type="verify">
  Your airline checks your documents before boarding and can refuse to fly you if they don't match the destination's requirements. Always verify with official government sources (and the airline) for your exact route.
</Callout>
