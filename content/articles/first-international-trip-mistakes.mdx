Everybody makes mistakes on their first international trip. Uncle made all of these, so you don't have to.

## Documents

1. **Passport validity too short.** Rules differ: the UAE requires six months, the Schengen area requires three months beyond departure plus a passport issued within the last 10 years, and South Africa requires 30 days plus two blank pages. Read [the six-month rule explained](/visas/guides/passport-validity-six-month-rule).
2. **Ignoring transit rules.** Connecting through another country can require a transit visa, in some cases even if you never leave the airport.
3. **Name mismatch.** Your ticket name should match your passport exactly.
4. **No copies.** Save photos of your passport, visa, insurance and bookings offline.
5. **Using a lookalike visa website.** Unofficial sites charge several times the official fee for ETAs and e-visas. Use government domains only. See [visa types explained](/visas/guides/visa-types-explained).

## Money

6. **One card only.** If it's blocked, you're stuck. Carry a backup on a different network.
7. **Not enabling international use.** Test your card before you go.
8. **Paying in your home currency.** Decline [dynamic currency conversion](/money/pay-in-local-currency-or-home-currency). In the EU, the markup must be shown on screen, so read it before you tap.
9. **Changing all your money at the airport.** NerdWallet reports airport premiums of around 14% over reference rates. [Here's why](/money/airport-currency-exchange).

## Connectivity & logistics

10. **Landing with no data plan.** Install an [eSIM](/travel-tech/best-esim-options-international-travelers) or know where to buy a SIM.
11. **Late-night arrival with no plan.** Pre-book the first night and know how you'll get there.
12. **Wrong plug.** Check [plug types and voltage](/travel-tech/power-adapters-voltage-explained). South Africa's Type M catches out many "universal" adapters.
13. **Power bank in the checked bag.** Spare lithium batteries must go in your carry-on (FAA).

## Health & timing

14. **Booking a big meeting the morning after a long-haul flight east.** The CDC says the body adjusts about 1 hour per day after flying east. See [how to beat jet lag](/guides/how-to-beat-jet-lag).

## Protection

15. **Skipping travel insurance**, or buying a policy without reading the exclusions. Some countries now require it: Georgia has required at least GEL 30,000 of medical cover for all visitors since 1 January 2026, and Schengen visa applicants need travel medical insurance.
