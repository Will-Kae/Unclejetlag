**What changed:** Kazakhstan has started rolling out QazETA, an electronic entry authorisation for foreign nationals who enter without a visa. Government Resolution No. 890, adopted on 10 October 2026, sets it up. Until 30 June 2027 it is recommended but not mandatory. From **1 July 2027** visa-free travellers will need it unless they fall into an exempt category.

## Who it affects

QazETA is for people who can already enter Kazakhstan visa-free, including citizens of Belarus. The foreign ministry's visa table (updated 21 August 2026) lists ordinary passports from the UK, Ireland, the US, Canada and Australia as visa-free for up to 30 days, along with many EU countries.

It does **not** replace a visa. The same table shows that ordinary passports from South Africa, Zimbabwe, Kenya, Nigeria, Botswana and Namibia still need a visa for Kazakhstan.

Exempt groups include holders of Kazakh residence permits, refugees and listed transport crews.

## How it works

- Apply through the QazETA app (App Store and Google Play) no later than 72 hours before entry.
- It is free and valid for 180 days.
- It does not change how long you may stay. Your visa-free stay limit still applies.
- Having one does not guarantee entry; border officers can still refuse.

## Key dates

- **1 November 2026:** rollout starts at Aktau airport and four road crossings.
- **1 January 2027:** Astana and Almaty airports.
- **1 March 2027:** all other border crossings.
- **1 July 2027:** QazETA becomes mandatory.

<Callout type="tip" title="Use the official app">
The notices point to the QazETA app in the official app stores. Be wary of websites offering to apply for a fee, as the authorisation itself is free.
</Callout>

## What to do now

1. Check whether your passport is visa-free for Kazakhstan on the foreign ministry's visa table.
2. If you travel before July 2027, applying is optional, but it may help you get used to the process.
3. From 1 July 2027, apply at least 72 hours before you arrive.
4. Confirm the details with the Kazakh authorities or your nearest Kazakh embassy before you travel.
