## At a glance

**Zimbabwean passport holders need a visa for Georgia.** For most travellers, that means the **e-visa**, applied for online at **evisa.gov.ge**. It gives short stays of **up to 30 days**.

**The shortcut:** if you already hold a **valid visa or residence permit** from a country on Georgia's official list, you can enter **visa-free for 90 days in any 180-day period**. The list has about 50 countries, including all EU/Schengen states, the UK, the US, Canada, Australia, Japan, the UAE, Saudi Arabia, Qatar and other Gulf states.

## Route 1: the e-visa

1. Go to **evisa.gov.ge**. It's the Georgian Ministry of Foreign Affairs portal. Ignore paid search ads for "Georgia visa" sites.
2. Fill in the form. Upload a passport scan and a compliant photo.
3. Pay about **US$20** plus a small card fee.
4. Allow **up to 5 working days**, then print the approved e-visa.

## Route 2: visa-free with another country's visa or permit

Georgia's government ordinance lets people holding a **visa and/or residence permit** from a listed country enter visa-free for **90 days in any 180**. The document must be **valid on the date you enter Georgia**. The list was last amended in April 2025, so check the current version on matsne.gov.ge.

## New for 2026: mandatory travel insurance

From **1 January 2026**, every foreign visitor must be able to show travel medical insurance at the border.

- **Minimum cover:** at least **GEL 30,000** (about US$11,000).
- **What it must cover:** emergency medical treatment, hospitalisation, emergency dental care, medical evacuation and repatriation.
- **If you don't have it:** you may have to buy a policy on the spot, or be refused entry.

## At the border

Carry printed copies of:

- Your e-visa or qualifying visa/permit
- Your insurance certificate
- Your return ticket and accommodation booking
- Proof of funds

Border officers make the final decision on entry.
