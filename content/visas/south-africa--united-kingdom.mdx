## At a glance

**South Africans need a visa to visit the UK.** South Africa is on the UK's *visa national list*, so you apply for a **Standard Visitor visa** before you travel. You can't just get an ETA.

A visa covers stays of up to 6 months per visit for tourism, visiting family and friends, business meetings, and some short courses.

## Fees (GOV.UK)

| Visa | Fee | Stay per visit |
|---|---|---|
| Standard Visitor (single application) | **£135** | up to 6 months |
| Long-term, 2 years | £506 | up to 6 months |
| Long-term, 5 years | £903 | up to 6 months |
| Long-term, 10 years | £1,128 | up to 6 months |

Optional priority services and courier fees are extra. **UK visa fees rise periodically**, so check GOV.UK on the day you pay.

## Timeline

- **Apply no earlier than 3 months before you travel.**
- Complete the online form on GOV.UK and pay.
- Book and attend a **biometrics appointment** at a UK visa application centre in South Africa. You'll give your fingerprints and photo there. You get your passport back the same day, but other documents may be kept during processing.
- **Decision:** usually within 3 weeks of that appointment.

<Callout type="tip">
  Peak seasons (June–August and December) get busy. For summer trips, aim to apply roughly 10–12 weeks ahead.
</Callout>

## Documents that make a strong application

GOV.UK asks for your travel plans, accommodation, trip costs, address history, employment and income, and your travel history for the past 10 years. In practice, a strong file includes:

1. A passport with a blank page.
2. Three to six months of bank statements that match the income you declared.
3. A letter from your employer confirming your job and approved leave. If you're self-employed, bring company registration and business bank statements instead.
4. Your accommodation booking, or an invitation letter with your host's UK immigration status and address.
5. A simple day-by-day plan and a cost estimate.

**What caseworkers are assessing:** whether you're a genuine visitor who will leave at the end of the trip, and whether you can pay for it. Every document should answer one of those two questions.

## Common refusal triggers

- Unexplained cash deposits shortly before applying
- Declared income that doesn't match your statements
- Not disclosing a previous refusal
- Vague plans ("tourism") with no accommodation or budget

## At the border

Carry printed copies of your accommodation, return ticket and invitation letter. Border Force can still refuse entry if your circumstances don't match your visa.
