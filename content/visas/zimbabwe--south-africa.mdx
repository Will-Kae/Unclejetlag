## At a glance

**Zimbabweans can visit South Africa without a visa for up to 90 days.** South Africa's official visa-exemption list gives Zimbabwe **90 days** for all passport types: diplomatic, official, service and ordinary. There's nothing to apply for in advance. You're admitted at the border post or airport.

<Callout type="warning">
  The exemption only covers **visits**: tourism, family, business meetings. You can't take up work or study on it. Overstaying can lead to you being declared "undesirable" and banned from returning for a period of time.
</Callout>

## What you need at the border

- **Passport validity:** at least **30 days after the date you plan to leave** South Africa.
- **Blank pages:** at least **2 consecutive completely blank visa pages**. Entry can be refused if you don't have them.
- **Return or onward ticket and accommodation details:** officers can ask for these.
- **Proof of funds:** bank statements or cards, if asked.
- **Children:** South Africa can ask for extra documents for minors, such as birth certificates and parental consent. Check Department of Home Affairs guidance before travelling with children.

## Cash and customs

You can bring in up to **R100,000** in foreign or local currency without prior approval; declare anything above that. Since 1 July 2026 you must also complete a **SARS online traveller declaration** no more than 24 hours before you cross ([what changed](/guides/south-africa-online-traveller-declaration-r100000-cash-limit)). Check SARS customs allowances before carrying goods for resale. Commercial quantities are dutiable.

## Crossing by road (Beitbridge)

Beitbridge is one of Africa's busiest land borders, and queues peak around month-end, Easter and December.

- Travel early in the day.
- Keep your documents together.
- Use only official border officials and facilities. Never hand documents to "runners."
